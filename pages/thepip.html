The Pip

The Pip is a Shortwave Radio Station that Tranmits at 5448 kHz at day, and 3756 kHz at night. Sister Stations like The Buzzer (UVB-76), and The Squeaky Wheel are Still Unconfirmed.

Country:Russia

Owner:Russian Armed Forces

Sister Station(s):UVB-76, The Squeaky Wheel (Unconfirmed)

Callsign:ЙВБ1

Frequency:5448/3756kHz

Confirmed it is Officially Affiliated with Russian Armed Forces, The Pip has grown in popularity. Having its First Air Date in Aug 1986, approx. ~16 years after UVB-76 First Appeared, with The Pip Firstly Recorded in 1986 and unconfirmed talk about tracing back ~1982.

The Pip Transmits short (repeated) beeps, approx. 50x/min @ 24hrs a day, interrupted occasionally by Russian voice messages.

More Information on The Pip can be Found on www.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pip OR www.priyom.org/military-stations/russia/the-pip
